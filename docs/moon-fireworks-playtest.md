# Moon Fireworks play-test checklist

A few things in Moon Fireworks can only be checked on a real phone. Try them on at least one
iPhone and one Android phone, and on an older phone if you have one. Each section says what to
look for, and the shortcuts below jump straight to the part being tested.

## Shortcuts

Add these to the end of the game's address, then tap **Launch the show**. You can combine them
with commas, for example `#candle,fps`.

| Add to the address | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `#fps` | Shows frames per second and the number of sparks in the bottom-left corner |
| `#effects` | Shows all 25 firework effects one at a time (tap for the next one) |
| `#candle` | Skips straight to a Roman candle break |
| `#drones` | Skips straight to the drone show intermission |

Example: `https://vortexfireworks.github.io/Vortex/moon-fireworks.html#candle,fps`

Games started with `#candle` or `#drones` aren't posted to the leaderboard, don't count as a
personal best and don't unlock achievements or effects.

## 1. Opening the game

- [ ] **Android:** tapping *Launch the show* switches to fullscreen (the browser's address bar and
      navigation buttons disappear).
- [ ] **iPhone:** in Safari, tap Share, then *Add to Home Screen*. Open the game from the new icon.
      It should open fullscreen, and the score bar shouldn't be hidden behind the notch or
      Dynamic Island.
- [ ] Turning the phone sideways and back doesn't leave a black or squashed screen.
- [ ] Switching to another app and back pauses the game.

## 2. Sound and vibration

- [ ] The van's engine and horn play while it drives in.
- [ ] Bursts sound like real fireworks bangs (sharp crack, then a rumble). The first one or two
      may still use the old sound while the recordings load. That's expected, but it shouldn't
      last longer than that.
- [ ] Big gold bursts sound bigger than regular ones.
- [ ] The bangs are loud enough over the music but not distorted during a grand finale.
- [ ] The mute button and the music button both work.
- [ ] **Android only:** the phone vibrates briefly when you pop a rocket. iPhones don't support
      vibration from web pages, so nothing happens there.

## 3. Smoothness (use `#fps`)

The counter shows green above 40 fps and red below.

- [ ] Normal play stays green.
- [ ] A grand finale stays mostly green.
- [ ] `#effects`: watch all 25 effects. Note any that turn the counter red. Spider, Snowflakes,
      Glitter and Brocade Crown are the busiest.
- [ ] `#drones,fps`: the drone show (about 4,500 lights) stays smooth.

If anything stutters, note the phone model and which effect or moment it was.

## 4. Roman candle (use `#candle`)

- [ ] Tapping the sky aims the candle and the star flies to where you tapped.
- [ ] Drones are possible but not too easy to shoot down. Note whether they feel too fast, too
      slow, too big or too small.
- [ ] Try each candle type over a few breaks (Classic, Thunder, Rainbow, Comet).
- [ ] The whippet hides behind the van and comes back out afterwards.
- [ ] A break feels about the right length (it lasts up to 14 seconds).

## 5. Drone show (use `#drones`)

- [ ] All four logos are recognisable.
- [ ] Tapping knocks drones down with a small explosion, and swiping across a logo knocks down
      every drone along the path.
- [ ] Show 6 starts after the last logo passes.

## 6. Scoring and goals

- [ ] A full normal game feels fair. The bonus rounds help, but the score mostly comes from popping
      rockets.
- [ ] A notice appears under the score bar when you see a new effect or unlock an achievement.
- [ ] *Effects* and *Achievements* on the menu show your progress, and it's still there after
      closing and reopening the game.
- [ ] Losing your last heart starts "Clear Your Boards!" with a 3-2-1 countdown, then every tap
      launches a shell. It lasts 10
      seconds (colours, glitter, crossettes, then salutes that are just a flash and a bang), and the
      phone keeps up with fast tapping.
- [ ] When a game ends, the After Action Report appears first, its numbers look right, and *Copy
      report* pastes into a message.
- [ ] Posting a score to the crew leaderboard works.

## What to send back

For anything that's off: the phone model, what happened, and a screenshot or screen recording if
you can.
