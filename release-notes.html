Release Notes

A running log of what's changed in Pyrotechnician Tools.

September 15, 2026

  • Fixed Check-In to show the same three separate license fields (Display, Special Effects, Flame) as the rest of the app, instead of the old single combined field
  • Added the same firework-icon design to HP Tools and Manage/Edit, so every page now matches the main screen's look
  • Phone number fields now consistently bring up the numeric keypad on mobile — fixed on Check-In, Manage Operators, and Update My Info (including emergency contact numbers)
  • Added "Undo" after removing someone from Payroll's crew list or the After-Action Report's personnel table
  • Added a "Download as CSV" option to Payroll, next to the PDF export
  • The After-Action Report now auto-saves your progress as a draft and offers to restore it if you leave and come back before finishing
  • Payroll and the After-Action Report now warn you before leaving the page if you have unsaved changes
  • Check-In now saves your submission on your device and sends it automatically once you're back online, if you check in without a signal

September 14, 2026

  • Added a "Head Pyro for this event" checkbox to Check-In, locked until you verify your phone number and confirm you have a license on file
  • Added a "Sign-In" shortcut inside HP Tools, linking to the same Check-In page
  • Payroll and the After-Action Report now auto-fill the Head Pyro / operator fields from whoever checked in with the Head Pyro box marked
  • Fixed Payroll's "Divide evenly between all people" so it actually includes the Head Pyro's amount in the split, instead of skipping it
  • Fixed a bug where loading check-ins into Payroll listed the Head Pyro twice — once in the Head Pyro field and again as a regular crew member
  • Fixed the "Back to main menu" link on HP Tools and Manage/Edit, which was sitting off to the left instead of above the page content
  • Made the "People & pay" table (Payroll) and the personnel table (After-Action Report) mobile-friendly — each person's fields now stack into a labeled card on narrow phone screens instead of squeezing into unreadably narrow columns

September 12, 2026

  • Split the single License field into three separate licenses — Display Operator (D), Special Effects Operator (P), and Flame Effects Operator (F) — across Manage Operators, New Hire Documents, and Update My Info
  • Check-In and the After-Action Report still show one combined License field, built automatically from whichever of the three someone has, to match the official state form
  • Added Emergency Contact name & phone, editable in Manage Operators and Update My Info, and now actually saved from the New Hire questionnaire (it was being asked for but not stored before)
  • Added a self-service "Update My Info" page — verify with your name and phone number on file, then update your own license, address, age, photo, and emergency contact without needing an admin password
  • Simplified passwords: HP Tools and Manage/Edit are now the only two locks in the app (Payroll, Manage Operators, and Manage Venues no longer have their own separate passwords)
  • Sped up phone verification on Check-In and fixed a photo-upload permissions issue behind the scenes
  • Added a "Description" field to Payroll that shows up for Shop Time, Bunker Time, and Trailer Loading, so you can note what was worked on
  • Email buttons (Payroll, After-Action Report, New Hire) now show the recipient address clearly on-screen and in the message text — the share sheet that attaches the PDF can't fill in the "To:" field automatically, so this makes it easy to catch and fill in yourself

September 11, 2026

  • Redesigned the whole app with a new dark, logo-themed look across every page
  • Added Manage Operators & Manage Venues photo/CSV tools, including bulk edit, CSV import/export, and a "View" option for operators
  • Added operator photo uploads (camera or file), viewable across the app
  • Added a New Hire Documents page with a questionnaire, photo upload, and links to required forms
  • Added Head Pyro tracking to Payroll, with an Hours mode for Shop Time/Bunker Time/Trailer Loading
  • Renamed Payroll's total section to "Budget" with a clearer running total
  • Switched email buttons to attach real PDFs via the share sheet on supported phones
  • Improved privacy on Check-In: phone number now verifies against your records before revealing license/address, and no crew data is stored in the page itself
  • Sped up the backend with caching so repeated lookups load faster
  • Fixed a bug where selecting a photo could get wiped out before saving
  • Added this Release Notes page
