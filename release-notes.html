Release Notes

A running log of what's changed in Pyrotechnician Tools.

October 5, 2026

  • The app is now called CueSheet, with a new name on every page, a new app icon, and a brief loading screen when you open it
  • Moved Manage/Edit from Head Pyro Tools to the main screen, as the last icon
  • Tidied Head Pyro Tools: removed the extra Sign-In button (Check-In is still on the main screen), and split the Check-In QR Code and Enable Sign-In into their own buttons, each on its own page. The QR code page links to Enable Sign-In and back, since crew can only check in to a show once sign-in is on for it
  • Improved Payroll Submit. Fixed several ways the amounts could go wrong without any warning: "Divide evenly" no longer gives a share of the money to an empty row, a total no longer counts an amount typed on a row with no name (a red note now says it won't be sent), a long crew no longer runs off the bottom of the PDF (it now continues on numbered pages), and an unused or hidden Head Pyro row can no longer inflate the total. Choosing a show now fills in its date and the crew who checked in, "Load people who checked in" adds new arrivals without erasing what you typed, and everything you enter is saved as you go so a locked phone or refresh doesn't lose it. Before anything is emailed or downloaded it checks for a missing venue or date, a person with no amount, a total that doesn't match, or a name listed twice, and afterward it confirms who it went to and what was sent
  • Reworked New Hire Documents. A three-step checklist with a progress bar now sits at the top (W-4, the ATF form, then the questionnaire) and remembers what you've ticked off. The ATF link now says exactly what it is: ATF Form 5400.28, the Explosives Employee Possessor Questionnaire. The questionnaire is split into short sections, starts with today's date, saves itself as you type so a locked phone or a refresh doesn't lose it, opens the front camera for your photo, lets your phone fill in your name, number, and email, and now asks for your age. Name and phone number are required, since the phone number is what lets Check-In recognize you. After you send it, the page tells you what happened and to check in with the same name and number. It also no longer overwrites anyone who is already on the crew: before, a current crew member who filled it in (Check-In sends people there when their number doesn't match) lost their licenses, address, age, and emergency contact
  • Made Check-In quicker. Once your name and phone number match what's on file, your license, address, and age tuck away behind a short "Found you" note (tap Review or edit to see them), so the Check in button is on the first screen. Tick "Remember me on this phone" and next time your name and number are already filled in: open the link, check the venue, tap Check in. "Not you?" clears it. The date and time now say "Checking in now" with a Change link, and the time recorded is when you tap Check in. The phone number box also now matches the other fields instead of showing up plain white
  • Added Enable Sign-In to Head Pyro Tools — pick which upcoming shows crew can sign in to. A show's sign-in stays open until two days after the event, then closes on its own. Check-In now only lists shows with sign-in turned on, plus Shop Time, Trailer Loading, and Bunker Time, which are always available
  • The same venue can now have more than one date — handy for a school you do twice in a season. Check-In and Payroll show each date as its own choice
  • Redesigned Manage Venues: each venue is listed once with all of its dates underneath, "+ Add another date" starts a new entry with the address already filled in, and every date now has its own Edit and Delete button. The form clearly says whether you're adding or editing, past dates are dimmed, the soonest upcoming show is listed first, and search now matches addresses and counties too. "Find duplicates" now only flags entries with the same name and the same date
  • After-Action Reports can now be saved. The report form has a Save to Drive button, and emailing a report saves a copy to Drive automatically. Saving the same report again replaces its copy instead of adding a second one. Find them again under Manage/Edit → Saved After Action Reports, where you can search by operator, venue, date, or license, filter by year, show only reports where something was reported, open the PDF, or delete one
  • Added Pick Lists. Build one for each show under Manage/Edit — shells by size and quantity, cakes, finale/quint/special racks, setup items, and a site map. Rack counts are worked out for you from the rack capacities you set once. Crews open the finished list under Pick Lists in Head Pyro Tools, check items off as they load, view the site map, and download or email a PDF
  • Every pick list now includes 26 permanent items you use on every show (E-match, batteries, cables in four lengths, radios, canopy, chairs, and more), alphabetized with a quantity box next to each
  • Manage Pick Lists can now copy a whole pick list from one show to another, for shows that are exactly the same. The site map isn't copied, since it belongs to the venue
  • The site map now shows near the top of the pick list PDF instead of on the last page, where it was easy to miss
  • Added Manage Inventory — track what's on hand by container (trailer, conex box, shelf) with each item's size, quantity, manufacturer, effects, and SKU. Scan SKU barcodes with your phone's camera on supported phones, and download a PDF with a signature line for whoever did the count
  • In Manage Inventory, items can now be moved to another container, pulled for a specific show, or removed (used, damaged, disposed of), each with an optional note. A Transaction History at the bottom of the page logs every move
  • Replaced Manage Firework Selection with Plan a Firework Show, a menu with three pages. Import Product Lists brings in your shell and cake catalogs from a CSV, with a downloadable template for each. Catalog lets you search everything by manufacturer, effect, colors, item number, SKU, or name, with assortment boxes grouped together. Plan a Show lets you create named show plans, add items from the Catalog, adjust quantities, and come back to them any time — everything saves automatically
  • The Pyro Directory now shows each person's photo, or their initials if they don't have one
  • Fixed Payroll not letting you submit when only the Head Pyro was entered — PDF, CSV, and email all work now
  • Fixed Manage Venues, Manage Operators, and other pages with text fields sliding sideways on phones
  • Fixed Payroll's Venue and Date of event fields running off the edge of the screen on phones when a venue has a long name
  • Enable Sign-In for an Event now saves automatically the moment you check or uncheck a box. Each row shows Saving… then Saved ✓, reads the sheet back to confirm the right date changed, and puts the box back with a message if it didn't go through
  • Fixed Shop Time, Trailer Loading, and Bunker Time each showing twice in Check-In and Payroll, and fixed Check-In jumping back to the first date of a venue when you had picked a later one
  • Fixed a behind-the-scenes issue where the app could occasionally show old or empty data from its offline storage instead of the latest from the sheet

  • Added an Event Date to Manage Venues, with the list sorted so the soonest upcoming show shows up first
  • Check-In's venue dropdown now shows "Date — Venue" combined and sorted by date, and only shows venues with a date set — except Shop Time, Trailer Loading, and Bunker Time, which are always available
  • Renamed "Date of event" to "Date of Check-In" on Check-In, and reordered the form so Name comes before Phone number, with a clearer note explaining what auto-fills
  • Fixed a bug where a venue's date could show up as "9/NaN" instead of the correct date
  • Added a pop-up on Check-In reminding first-timers to fill out New Hire Documents before signing in, if their name and phone don't match anyone on file
  • Fixed Payroll's venue dropdown, which wasn't using event dates at all — it now shows venues within 3 months of today, sorted soonest first, the same way Check-In does
  • Added "Autofill from Sign-In" to the After-Action Report — pick a real event from Check-In records and it fills in the date, venue, and the Head Pyro's name and license automatically
  • Venue CSV import now recognizes more header name variants for the event date column

  • Fixed a bug where renaming someone (or a venue) created a duplicate entry instead of updating the existing one — renaming now updates the same record in place
  • Added the "Russ Check" to Check-In — license number fields now require the format "FE" followed by numbers, with an on-screen message if an entry doesn't pass
