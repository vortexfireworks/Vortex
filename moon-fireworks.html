Moon Fireworks

You're the only one on the moon for the big celebration. Rockets are launching, but their fuses are broken!

Click a rocket before it reaches the top to make it explode. Let 5 rockets fizzle and the show is over.

Explosions can set off nearby rockets for chain bonuses. Gold rockets are worth extra, and pink rockets give you a life back. Watch for the rare dark red rocket!
