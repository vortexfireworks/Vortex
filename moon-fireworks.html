Moon Fireworks

You're the only crew on the moon for the big celebration, and the rockets' fuses are broken!

Tap a rocket before it reaches the top to set it off. Beat each show's target for a grand finale.

How to play

Tap rockets before they reach the top. Every rocket that fizzles costs a ♥, and when all 5 are gone the show's over.

  • Regular: 10 points. Bursts as a peony, chrysanthemum, palm, crossette, willow, crackle or ring.
  • ★Gold: 50 points.
  • ♥Pink: gives you a life back.
  • UDark red: rare! 100 points.
  • 2Two-stage: splits into 3 mini rockets. Tap those too, 15 points each.
  • ⏱Blue: slows every rocket down for 6 seconds.
  • ✸Purple: mega burst that sets off every rocket on screen.
  • ✕Grey dud: don't tap it! It costs 25 points. Let it fizzle, it's harmless.

Bursts set off nearby rockets for chain bonuses, and 10 in a row raises your multiplier. Each show you finish earns a grand finale and a bonus ♥. Tap the showman once per show for a surprise.
