Moon Fireworks

You're the only crew on the moon for the big celebration, and the rockets' fuses are broken!

Tap a rocket before it reaches the top to set it off. Beat each show's target for a grand finale.

How to play

Tap rockets before they reach the top. Every rocket that fizzles costs a ♥, and when all 5 are gone the show's over.

  • Regular: 10 points. Bursts as one of 25 classic effects, from peony, palm tree and crossette to spider, jellyfish, silver dragon and time rain.
  • ★Gold: 50 points.
  • ♥Pink: gives you a life back.
  • UDark red: rare! 100 points.
  • YBright blue: rare! 100 points, and someone drops in to say hello.
  • 2Two-stage: splits into 3 mini rockets. Tap those too, 15 points each.
  • ⏱Blue: slows every rocket down for 6 seconds.
  • ✸Purple: mega burst that sets off every rocket on screen.
  • ✕Grey dud: don't tap it! It costs 25 points. Let it fizzle, it's harmless.
  • ✈Drone: rare! It flies across the sky with blinking lights. Don't tap it! It costs 1000 points.

Bursts set off nearby rockets for chain bonuses, and 10 in a row raises your multiplier. Each show you finish earns a grand finale and a bonus ♥. When your last ♥ goes, Chuck shouts "Clear Your Boards!": tap as fast as you can to launch everything that's left, 5 points a shell.

Bonus rounds

  • 🕯Roman candle: partway through most shows. Tap the sky to aim each shot.
  • ✈Drones during the candle: fair game! Shoot them down for 100 points each.
  • 🛸Drone show: after show 5. Tap or swipe across the drones to knock them down, 1 point for every 3.

Tap Chuck once per show for a surprise. His whippet tags along too, so watch where you step. Now and then a stargazer wanders out to point out the planets; tap him to see the next one. Collect all 25 effects and unlock achievements from the menu.
