Manage/Edit

Manage Operators

Add a new operator or update an existing one's license, phone, address, age, or photo — changes show up for autofill everywhere.

Manage Venues

Add venues and their dates, edit or delete them, or add another date for a venue you already have — changes show up in the venue lists everywhere.

Manage Pick Lists

Build the pick list for a show: shells, cakes, racks, setup items, and a site map. Crews see it under Pick Lists in Head Pyro Tools.

Manage Inventory

Track what's on hand by container — shells, cakes, manufacturer, effects, and SKU. Scan SKUs with your camera and export a signed PDF anytime.

Manage Catalog

Add or edit products by hand, import a whole price list from a CSV, or find and remove duplicates.

Saved After Action Reports

Look up every After-Action Report saved to Drive — search by operator, venue, date, or license, open the PDF, or flag the ones with incidents.

Announcements

Post a message that shows as a banner on the home screen, and remove old ones.

Backups

Keep dated copies of the whole spreadsheet in Drive, see when the last one ran, or take one now.
