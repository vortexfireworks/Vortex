Privacy Policy — Vortex CueSheet

Vortex CueSheet is an internal scheduling and operations tool used by Vortex Fireworks Artists crew members. This page explains what information the app handles.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address to identify you and match you to your crew role.
  • Information you submit directly: show sign-ups, availability, payroll and check-in records, after-action reports, and photos submitted to the Photo Wall.

How it's used

This information is used only to run day-to-day crew operations — scheduling shows, tracking payroll, and crew communication. It is not sold or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Who can see it

Access is role-based: crew members see what their role is granted, and administrators manage the full system. Data is stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive under the organization's own Google account.

Questions

Contact vortexfireworksartists@gmail.com with any questions about this policy.
