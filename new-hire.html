Trouble emailing?

On phones that support it, "Email to Vortex" opens your share sheet with the actual PDF ready to attach. The share sheet can't fill in the recipient for you, so double-check the "To:" field once Mail opens — use "Copy address" above if it helps. On devices that don't support this, it downloads the PDF and opens a plain email instead, and you'll need to attach the PDF yourself.
